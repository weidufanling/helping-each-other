START
Read the opening page, then use Small Start, Need or Offer, Consent and Contact,
and Matching Proposal for one modest trial. The contents page gives page numbers
for every guide and printable blank. Use the remaining cards when needed.

PRINTING AND EDITING
Print the PDF at actual size on A4 paper. Each blank worksheet has its own page.
The Word document is editable; no form lock or password is applied. Duplicate a
worksheet before reusing it. Blank response lines are writing spaces, not text
that must be preserved when editing. The PDF is printable, not an interactive
form. No paid software subscription or special account is required to use
the kit. Readers may use compatible free document and spreadsheet applications.

EXTRA CONNECTIONS
The browser helpers fill prepared worksheets using your own words, so they need
no API key or MCP connection. The printable kit works independently on paper or
in ordinary document software. Connections can be useful when a task needs
outside information or actions; these worksheets do not need them.

THE ACTION TRACKER
Record tasks only after the person has accepted. Use an agreed label rather than
private contact details. Suggested states: proposed, discussing, agreed, paused,
withdrawn, complete. A blank cell is not agreement. Keep contact choices in a
separate place and agree when to remove them. The CSV contains no participant
details or example records.

SCOPE
Harbour Lane Book Share and all named people in its examples are fictional.
They are not customers, testimonials or endorsements. The worksheets support
small, low-stakes coordination. They do not assess suitability, certify safety,
replace professional support or require anyone to participate. No financial
outcome is promised by these worksheets.

REUSE
You may edit and copy the blank worksheets for use in your own group or activity.
Keep the attribution with copies of the guide. Do not imply endorsement by the
author or by the fictional participants.
