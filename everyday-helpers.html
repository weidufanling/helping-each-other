How does this work without extra connections?

Some tasks need outside services. These helpers use prepared templates and the words you enter, processed in your browser. You do not need an API key, MCP connection or special account. Connections can be useful when a task needs outside information or actions; they are not needed for these worksheets.

What can I expect?

You can find a suitable worksheet, prepare an editable draft and save it to your device. The suggestions match words in your request to the available helpers. They do not check facts independently or make professional decisions. You remain responsible for checking the details and deciding whether to use the result.
