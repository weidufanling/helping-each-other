Helping Each Other

Community Kit

A practical coordination kit for small groups.

Eight reusable worksheets help a group try something together. Use them to identify needs and offers, agree contact choices, propose a match, record decisions, consider consequences, repair problems and review what happened.

Each worksheet includes instructions, a blank version and a worked fictional example. Use it on paper or in ordinary document software; no subscription or special account is needed.

Inside the kit

  • 1. Small Start
  • 2. Need or Offer
  • 3. Consent and Contact
  • 4. Matching Proposal
  • 5. A Short Meeting That Ends in a Decision
  • 6. The Consequence and Missing Voice Check
  • 7. A Problem and Repair Conversation
  • 8. Review, Close or Hand Over
