# Helping Each Other

[Use the free helpers and Community Kit at Weidu](https://weidufanling.au/helping-each-other/).

Ten browser worksheets help with communication, creative work, community questions and learning. They organise the words you supply into drafts you can edit. They use prepared templates, not a remote AI model, and submit no messages or requests. Check facts, permissions and missing details before sharing.

## Open locally

Open `everyday-helpers.html` in an ordinary browser. `small-start.html` offers a bounded first step, and `kit-guide.html` explains the Community Kit. No account, payment, donation, API key or special connection is required.

The Community Kit contains eight worksheets with instructions, blanks and a fictional book-share example. Use the editable Markdown, Word document, blank CSV tracker or printable 18-page PDF. The PDF and Word files preserve the original public edition. The Markdown has a wording and punctuation review dated 29 October 2026; it preserves the same activities, consent choices and examples.

Only the selected public helpers and kit are included. The underlying private source catalogue, original GPT instructions, internal provenance notes and private working records are excluded. Named participants in the worked examples are fictional.

## Reuse and scope

The existing permission allows editing and copying the blank worksheets for your own group or activity, with attribution retained on copies of the guide and no implied endorsement. This repository preserves that permission; it does not add a blanket software or content licence. See `REUSE.md` and the original `README.txt`.

Use invented or non-sensitive examples. Completed worksheets, contact details and real participant records do not belong in this repository or its issues. These are tools for small, low-stakes coordination, not suitability assessments, safety certifications or replacements for professional support.
